1. Project Overview & Commercial Vision

Product Architecture

DigMitra PRO is a mobile-first, real-time heavy-machinery booking marketplace designed to bridge the gap between property owners/citizens and verified JCB/Excavator/Tipper Truck operators. Similar to how Uber/Rapido revolutionized passenger transport, DigMitra enables any citizen to pinpoint their plot via live hardware GPS, inspect nearby available JCBs, upload site photos, choose a payment mode, pay a nominal ₹500 token advance, and track their dispatched JCB live on a turn-by-turn map.

Target Market & Use Cases

  • Plot Clearing & Bush Removal (30×40 ft, 40×60 ft, 60×40 ft residential/commercial sites)
  • Construction Debris Extraction (JCB Backhoe + Tractor Tipper haulage combos)
  • Foundation Trenching & Site Leveling
  • Primary Hub: Kyatasandra, Tumakuru & Bengaluru, Karnataka

Multi-Role & Bilingual Capabilities

  • 3 User Roles: Client/Consumer, JCB Fleet Owner, and Marketplace Admin
  • Bilingual UI: Instant 1-tap switch between English and Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ)
  • Anti-Fraud Token Model: ₹500 advance online lock + remaining balance via Cash on Spot or Online

5. End-to-End Consumer Booking Architecture (7-Stage Funnel)

User Journey
1

Login & Instant OTP Verification (app/components/auth/login-screen.tsx)

Displays the clean welcome header ("Hello Client...!! Ready to book JCB..." with custom JCB icon), collects Name and 10-digit Mobile Number (+91), generates a 4-digit OTP with an interactive SMS Banner & 1-click Auto-Fill, and includes a plain-text "Log-in as JCB Owner" mode switcher at the bottom.

2

Live Hardware GPS Map & JCB Discovery (app/components/map/realtime-map.tsx)

Automatically acquires the user's real-time hardware location using a 3-tier GPS engine (Satellite GNSS → Network Wi-Fi Fix → Calibrated Kyatasandra Hub). Displays nearby JCBs with live ETA badges, supports Vector/Satellite switching, and presents the bottom confirmation card with the prominent yellow "BOOK JCB →" button.

3

Site Booking Details & Live Plot Camera (app/components/booking/site-booking-details.tsx)

Allows the consumer to select plot dimensions (30 × 40 ft, 40 × 60 ft, 60 × 40 ft, or custom), adjust estimated machine hours, toggle Tractor Tipper debris removal trips, and capture/upload surrounding plot photos via the live camera modal.

4

Payment Mode Selection (app/components/booking/payment-mode-screen.tsx)

Lets the consumer choose how they want to settle the main work bill: Cash on Spot (pay operator directly at the site after work completion) or Online Payment (UPI / NetBanking / Cards).

5

JCB Machine Catalog & Operator Selection (app/components/booking/jcb-catalog-screen.tsx)

Presents available machinery near the site: JCB 3DX Super Backhoe (₹950/hr), JCB 3DX EcoXpert (₹1,050/hr), JCB 2DX Mini Backhoe (₹800/hr), and Heavy Tracked Excavator (₹1,450/hr) with operator ratings, registration numbers, and ETAs.

6

₹500 Token Advance Payment Gateway (app/components/booking/advance-payment-screen.tsx)

Collects a refundable/adjustable ₹500 Advance Booking Fee via PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm, or UPI ID to confirm genuine intent and lock the JCB operator. Automatically deducts ₹500 from the total estimated bill.

7

Booking Confirmation & Live Turn-by-Turn Tracking (booking-success-screen.tsx & live-tracking-screen.tsx)

Displays the high-visibility green/yellow confirmation modal with Booking ID (#JCB-XXXX), triggers an in-app push notification with sound, and launches the live route tracker showing the JCB moving in real time toward the consumer's plot.
